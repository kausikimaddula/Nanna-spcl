🎵 HOW TO ADD YOUR SONG / AUDIO:
================================

1. Place your song file inside this folder:
   c:\Users\SAI KAUSIKI\Downloads\Nanna-spcl\public\audio\

2. Name the file:
   song.mp3

   (You can use .mp3, .m4a, .wav, .ogg, or .aac)

3. That's it!
   The website is connected and will automatically play your song when Nanna draws the bow or taps the music button!

4. (Optional) Customizing:
   If your song has a different filename or you want to adjust the volume, 
   open `audio-data.js` in the project root to edit the settings.
